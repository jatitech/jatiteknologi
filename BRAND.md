## Strategy

### Overview

Jatiteknologi is the trading name of **PT Jati Teknologi Indonesia**, a software and
IT services company registered in Kabupaten Tangerang, Banten, in October 2023. It
builds, implements, and maintains business systems for Indonesian small and
medium businesses: custom software, ERP (ERPNext), and IT advice and support.

Origin: the company was founded in 2023 by Risqi Romadhoni, a self-taught engineer
who has worked in software since 2016. It stayed dormant while the founder worked as a
freelancer and employee, and this brand marks its first public launch.

What it really does: it moves a business from scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp
threads, and paper notes into one system the owner can trust, and then keeps
that system working for years.

The structural problem: SME software in Indonesia is usually bought one of two
ways. Cheap builds are handed over and abandoned, so the system decays the first
time the business changes. Enterprise vendors are priced and paced for companies
with an IT department. A growing business ends up with software it can neither
afford to fix nor rely on.

Transformation:
- **Before:** stock, orders, and money live in five places; the owner is the only
  person who knows the real numbers; the last app vendor stopped answering.
- **After:** one system holds the records, staff can use it without the owner in
  the room, the code and data belong to the business, and the engineer who built
  it is still reachable.

Long-term ambition: be the engineering partner Indonesian SMEs keep for a decade,
the way a family keeps good teak furniture.

### Audience

**Primary: owners and operations leads of Indonesian SMEs (UMKM)** with roughly 5
to 100 staff, such as distributors, retailers with several outlets, small
manufacturers, and service businesses. They run on Excel, WhatsApp, and notebooks,
and they feel it when stock counts don't match, invoices go out late, or the
business can't grow beyond what the owner can watch personally.

- **They already believe:** a proper system would help; they have seen
  competitors or suppliers use one.
- **They are skeptical of:** vendors who vanish after handover, quotes full of
  jargon, monthly fees that keep rising, and "transformasi digital" talk that
  never touches daily work.
- **What they want:** fewer manual reconciliations, numbers they can trust, and
  someone they can call who understands their system.

**Secondary:**
- **Founders of small companies and startups** who need a senior engineer on
  contract for a web app, an internal tool, or an integration. They read
  technical detail and care about stack choice and code quality.
- **Individual clients** (the founder's current freelance base), who now get a
  registered company as the contracting party, with company invoices and
  contracts.

**Not for:**
- Enterprises and government tenders that require large teams, formal
  certifications, or 24/7 SLAs the company cannot honestly offer today.
- Buyers looking for the cheapest template website or app clone.
- Information-security consulting, digital identity, or electronics
  installation work. Those KBLI codes still lack a verified Sertifikat Standar
  (see Governance > Claims).

### Positioning

**Category:** A founder-led software studio that builds and maintains business
systems for Indonesian SMEs (*mitra sistem bisnis untuk UMKM*).

**What it is not:** Not a cheap app-making service (*jasa bikin aplikasi*). Not a
SaaS reseller. Not a big systems integrator. Not a staffing agency. Not an "AI
agency."

**Competitive landscape:** SMEs choose between three layers:
1. **Off-the-shelf local SaaS** (accounting, POS, inventory subscriptions). Fast
   to start, but rigid: the business bends to the software, and data sits on
   someone else's terms.
2. **Freelancers and small agencies.** Affordable and flexible, but often gone
   after handover, with little documentation and code the client can't take
   elsewhere.
3. **Large system integrators and ERP vendors.** Capable, but priced, staffed,
   and paced for enterprises.

Jatiteknologi sits between layers 2 and 3: senior engineering and long-term care,
delivered at SME scale by a registered company.

**Structural differentials:**
- **The person who scopes the work builds it.** The founder is a senior
  full-stack engineer (professional since 2016), so nothing gets lost between
  sales and delivery.
- **Open foundations, no lock-in.** Built on open-source ERPNext/Frappe and
  standard stacks (TypeScript, PHP/Laravel). The client owns the code, the data,
  and the documentation.
- **Built to be maintained.** Every system ships with documentation and a support
  path, because the company plans to still be the one maintaining it.
- **A registered PT, not an individual.** PT Jati Teknologi Indonesia (NIB
  0410230086416) contracts and invoices as a company.
- **Bilingual by default.** Indonesian first, English when the client's team or
  stack needs it.

**Territory owned:** *Keawetan*, durability. Teak (*jati*) is chosen because it
lasts. Jatiteknologi builds systems for the same reason.

### Personality

**Attributes:** Durability, Care, Clarity, Accountability, Warmth.

- **Inkeep (inkeep.com):** Borrow its warm off-white ground, soft sky-blue washes

  labels that make a site feel engineered rather than decorated. Do not borrow

  its proprietary display face.

  honest joinery and pride in finish. Do not borrow ornate carving, brown
  wood-grain textures, or rustic "heritage" styling.
- **Basecamp / 37signals:** Borrow its plain-spoken voice, its willingness to say
  "we're small and that's the point," and its calm, opinionated explanations.
  Do not borrow its combative, anti-industry tone.
- **heyris.me (the founder's site):** Borrow its Indonesian-English parity, its
  WCAG 2.2 AA standard, and its no-tracking stance. Do not borrow its personal,
  diary-like register or its neutral-only palette.

- **"Jasa pembuatan aplikasi murah" sites:** Reject handshake stock photos,
  "harga mulai Rp…!" banners, made-up testimonials, countdown timers, and
  pop-up WhatsApp spam.
- **Enterprise systems integrator brochures:** Reject "transformasi digital
  end-to-end," "solusi holistik," city-skyline stock photos, and certification
  badges nobody can verify.
- **AI-hype startups:** Reject neon purple gradients, robot and brain imagery,
  and "AI-powered" stuck onto services that don't use AI.

### Tonal Rules

1. **English source, Indonesian default.** Draft copy in English, then translate
   it with the `bahasa-indonesia-plain` skill. Indonesian is the default locale on
   public sites. Both versions carry the same claims, and neither mixes languages
   inside a sentence, except for established terms such as ERP or server.
2. **Address readers as "you" / "Anda."** Refer to the company as "we" / "kami."
   Never use "lo/gue" or "kalian" for clients.
3. **Talk about operations before technology.** Say "your stock matches the
   warehouse" before "inventory module."
4. **One idea per sentence.** Keep sentences between 12 and 20 words, and no longer
   than 25 words.
5. **Be honest about size.** Say "we're a small company" where it matters. Never
   imply a team, clients, or history the company doesn't have.
6. **Put numbers before adjectives.** If there is no number, use a concrete
   example instead of "fast," "reliable," or "best."
7. **Explain cost and scope up front.** Don't hide pricing logic behind "contact
   us" when an estimate is possible.
8. **Credit open source.** Name ERPNext, Frappe, and other tools as what they are;
   never present them as our own product.
9. **Use the teak metaphor sparingly.** At most once per page, and only where it
   explains something: durability, phases, or handing down.
10. **No pressure tactics.** No countdowns, "limited slots," or fake urgency.
11. **Write calm headlines.** No exclamation marks in headlines; at most one per
    page, and only to celebrate something real.
12. **Follow Indonesian conventions in ID copy.** Write "Rp75.000", "4 Oktober
    2023", "10.00", and "10 hingga 20 orang". Don't use "&", "dll.", or "e.g."

## Visual

### Logo & Marks

*Chosen on 17 November 2026: "Tenon JT", concept B of five. The founder
delegated the choice. A trademark search is still open.*

- **Symbol:** the J and T of Jati Teknologi, joined like a mortise and tenon. The
  T bar and the J are Jati Blue. The tenon that joins them is Teak, the only
  Teak in the logo. The joint stands for what the studio builds: systems that fit
  a business and hold.
- **Wordmark:** "Jatiteknologi" in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, sentence case,
  Ink, drawn as outlines.
- **Lockups:** symbol + wordmark (horizontal) as the primary; stacked for square
  spaces; symbol alone for favicons and avatars; wordmark alone when the symbol
  is already on screen. A heavier small-size symbol is used at 32 px and below.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the symbol's T bar on every side.
- **Prohibitions:** never stretch, rotate, or outline the mark; never recolor it
  outside Ink, Jati Blue, Teak, and white; never put it on a wood-grain texture or
  a busy photo; never add shadows, glows, or gradients to the mark itself.
- **Source files:** `brand/logo/` holds the SVG masters, and
  `brand/logo/export/` holds the variants and web icons. `brand/logo/GUIDELINES.md`
  covers sizes, colours and handover notes.
- **Rejected:** the growth-ring proposal (three quarter-arcs and a dot) read as an
  RSS or Wi-Fi icon. Round-1 concepts are kept in `brand/logo/concepts/`.

### Core Colors

*Proposed. Values are contrast-checked; the meanings await founder approval.*

- **Jati Blue** `#1D5FD6` (mandatory). The "teknologi" half of the name: clarity,
  signal, and trust. It is deeper than the category's common light azure, so
  white text on it reaches 5.7:1.
- **Ink** `#0E1A2B` (mandatory). A blue-black for the brand's voice and the
  wordmark. It is permanent and calm, and never pure black.
- **Teak** `#9C5A22` (optional, scarce). The "jati" half: warmth, craft, the
  heartwood. Its meaning depends on rarity, so it appears in small doses, never
  as a large fill.
- **Paper** `#FAF8F4` (optional). A warm, unbleached ground for when pure white
  feels clinical, like a draftsman's sheet.

Avoid: neon or electric gradients, purple "AI" gradients, wood-texture browns as
backgrounds, pure black, and any green that reads as a WhatsApp clone.
